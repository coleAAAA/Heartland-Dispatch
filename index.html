Questions

Straight answers

Do I have to change my phone number or forward calls?

No. Keep your number exactly as it is. You only add one sentence to your voicemail greeting and website telling callers to dial our number and enter yours.

What does my customer have to do?

Call the number from your voicemail greeting, key in your 10-digit phone number, and say what's wrong after the beep. They hear your company name before they leave the message, so they know they reached the right place.

What if my phone goes to voicemail when you call?

You also get an email with the caller's number, the recording, and a call-back button, so you still have the message. Save 615-640-8506 in your contacts so the alert call is recognized.

Which phones ring?

Your main number plus up to five backup numbers, all at the same time. Add your office, your on-call tech, or your spouse.

Are the calls recorded?

Yes. That's how you hear the message. Callers are told the call is recorded before the beep, and they can hang up if they don't want that. Recording laws vary by state, so see our Terms.

Is this a 911 or emergency service?

No. It's a message-capture and alert tool for contractors. The caller is told at the start of the call to hang up and dial 9-1-1 if anyone is in danger.

How do I cancel?

Email coleeritter@gmail.com from the address on your account. There's no contract. Your service continues until the end of the period you've paid for.
