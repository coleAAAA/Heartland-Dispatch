Privacy Policy

Last updated November 2026

The short version: We collect what we need to run your line: your business details, the caller’s number, and the recorded message. We do not sell personal information. You can ask us to delete anything by email.

This policy explains what information Heartland Dispatch collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. It covers our website and the automated call-capture service.

1. Information we collect

From contractors (account holders):

  • Company name, owner email address, primary on-call phone number, and any backup phone numbers you enter.
  • Billing information, which is collected and processed by Stripe. We receive a customer ID and subscription status, not your full card number.

From callers who use the dispatch line:

  • The caller’s phone number (caller ID).
  • The contractor number the caller keys in.
  • An audio recording of the message the caller leaves. Callers hear a notice that the call is recorded before recording begins.
  • Call date and time, and a log of whether the alert was delivered to the contractor.

Automatically: our hosting and security providers may log technical information such as IP address and browser type when you visit the website.

2. How we use information

  • To provide the Service: look up the contractor, place alert calls, and send alert emails with the recording link.
  • To process payments, manage subscriptions, and suspend accounts that are unpaid or canceled.
  • To troubleshoot problems, prevent misuse, and keep the Service secure.
  • To contact you about your account, support requests, and important changes.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use caller recordings for advertising.

3. Who we share information with

We share information only with service providers that help us run the Service, and only as needed for that purpose:

  • Telnyx for phone calls and recordings.
  • Resend for sending alert emails.
  • Supabase for database storage of account information.
  • Stripe for payments.
  • Vercel and Cloudflare for hosting and security.

The contractor you reach receives the caller’s phone number and recording. We may also disclose information if required by law, to respond to legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users or the public. If our business is sold or merged, information may transfer to the new owner.

4. Call recording

Calls to the dispatch line are recorded so the message can be delivered to the contractor. A notice is played before recording starts. If you do not want to be recorded, hang up when you hear the notice and contact the contractor directly.

5. Retention

We keep account information while your account is active and for a reasonable time afterward for billing, legal, and security purposes. Recordings and call details are kept for as long as needed to provide the Service and resolve disputes, and you can ask us to delete them (see below).

6. Security

We use reputable providers and reasonable safeguards, including encrypted connections and restricted access to our database. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Recording links in alert emails should be treated as private, so please do not forward them to people who should not hear the call.

7. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to delete a specific recording, by emailing coleeritter@gmail.com. Depending on where you live (for example, California and several other states), you may have additional rights, including the right to know what we collect, to request deletion or correction, and not to be discriminated against for exercising your rights. We will verify your request and respond within the time the law requires. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

8. Children

The Service is intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows the latest version. If a change is material, we will notify you by email or on the website.

10. Contact

Questions or requests: coleeritter@gmail.com.
